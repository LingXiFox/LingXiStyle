隐私政策

纹粒 / Texel · 版本 1.9.1 · 最后更新：2026-11-12

本政策适用于 iOS App「纹粒 / Texel」（Bundle ID: com.lingxifox.Texel），包括 App、分享扩展与快捷指令。

摘要：开发者不运营用于存储照片或账号的服务器。照片只在你明确选择时通过 PhotoKit 访问，并在设备本机处理。App 不包含广告、分析 SDK 或跨 App 跟踪。免费额度计数保存在本机 Keychain；Pro 权益由 Apple StoreKit 验证。

1. 我们访问的照片

当你选择照片或从「文件」导入时，App 会读取该文件的原始 HEIC / HEIF 资源，用于在设备本机追加纹理与颗粒。转换结果通过 PhotoKit 另存为相册中的新照片；原照片不会被修改、覆盖或删除。

保存时需要「添加到照片」权限；读取时需要照片访问权限。你可以随时在 iOS「设置」中撤销权限。撤销后仍可通过「文件」导入使用不需要相册读取权限的功能。

2. 本机数据

  • 临时文件：转换过程中的工作文件位于 App 沙盒的临时目录，任务结束或清理后删除。
  • 免费额度：本机 Keychain 保存当日已用 / 预留的槽位计数，不包含照片内容、账号或位置。
  • Pro 权益：购买与恢复由 Apple StoreKit 处理；App 只读取经过验证的交易与当前权益。
  • 成果保险箱与任务历史：转换结果与任务记录保存在 App 沙盒，不参与 iCloud 备份；删除 App 时一并删除。免费体验凭证保存在本机 Keychain，不包含照片内容。

删除 App 可能不会立即删除 Keychain 中的额度计数；重新安装后该计数可能仍存在。照片结果由系统「照片」App 管理，直到你自行删除。

3. 网络与第三方

开发者不接收你的照片，也不运营照片上传服务。以下系统流程可能使用网络：iCloud 照片原件由 PhotoKit 从 Apple iCloud 下载；购买、恢复购买与交易验证由 Apple StoreKit 处理。这些流程受 Apple 的隐私政策与条款约束。

本 App 不集成第三方分析、广告、崩溃上报或社交 SDK。分享操作由 iOS 系统分享面板完成，你选择的接收方由其自身隐私政策约束。

4. 收集与跟踪

开发者不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码、精确位置、通讯录、广告标识符或照片内容，不进行跨 App 跟踪，不出售个人信息。App Store 的下载、购买与退款数据由 Apple 处理。

5. 儿童

App 不面向儿童设计，不包含账号、社交或用户生成内容功能，也不主动收集儿童个人信息。

6. 你的选择与权利

你可以在 iOS 设置中管理照片权限，在 App 设置中查看 Pro 状态与恢复购买，或通过系统删除已保存的照片。若你所在司法辖区赋予你访问、更正、删除或反对处理个人信息的权利，可通过下方联系方式提出；由于开发者不持有你的照片或账号，通常无法代为访问设备上的内容。

功能或法律要求变化时，本政策可能更新。重大变更会在本页面标注新的更新日期；继续使用 App 前请查看最新版本。

Privacy Policy

Texel · Version 1.9.1 · Last updated: 2026-11-12

This policy applies to the iOS app Texel (Bundle ID: com.lingxifox.Texel), including its Share Extension and Shortcuts actions.

Summary: The developer does not operate a server that stores photos or accounts. Photos are accessed through PhotoKit only when you explicitly select them and are processed on your device. The app contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. The free-quota counter is stored in the local Keychain; Pro entitlement is verified by Apple StoreKit.

1. Photos we access

When you select a photo or import a file, the app reads the original HEIC / HEIF resource to add texture and grain on your device. The converted result is saved through PhotoKit as a new photo in your library. The original photo is never modified, overwritten, or deleted.

Saving requires the Add to Photos permission; reading requires photo-library access. You can revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings. After revocation, you can still use the Files import path for features that do not require photo-library read access.

2. On-device data

  • Temporary files: Working files are kept in the app sandbox's temporary directory and are deleted when a task ends or is cleaned up.
  • Free quota: The local Keychain stores the day's used / reserved slot count. It contains no photo content, account, or location.
  • Pro entitlement: Purchases and restores are handled by Apple StoreKit. The app only reads verified transactions and current entitlements.
  • Vault and task history: Converted results and task records are stored in the app sandbox and are excluded from iCloud backup; deleting the app deletes them. The free-trial receipt is stored in the local Keychain and contains no photo content.

Deleting the app may not immediately remove the quota counter from the Keychain; it may persist after reinstalling. Converted photos are managed by the system Photos app until you delete them.

3. Network and third parties

The developer does not receive your photos and does not operate a photo-upload service. The following system flows may use the network: PhotoKit downloads iCloud originals from Apple iCloud, and Apple StoreKit handles purchases, restores, and transaction verification. Those flows are governed by Apple's privacy policy and terms.

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs. Sharing is handled by the iOS system share sheet; the recipient you choose is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Collection and tracking

The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, advertising identifier, or photo content; does not track you across apps; and does not sell personal information. App Store download, purchase, and refund data is handled by Apple.

5. Children

The app is not designed for children and does not include accounts, social features, or user-generated content. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices and rights

You can manage photo permissions in iOS Settings, view Pro status and restore purchases in the app's settings, or delete saved photos through the system Photos app. If your jurisdiction gives you rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of personal information, you may contact us below. Because the developer does not hold your photos or an account, it generally cannot access content on your device on your behalf.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when features or legal requirements change. Material changes will be marked with a new update date on this page. Please review the latest version before continuing to use the app.
